#### 7.5 Limits of inference

| Sentence | Sources |
|---|---|
| Aggregated exposure paired with aggregated prevalence cannot establish that the exposed people within an area are the affected people. |  |
| That is the ecological fallacy, and it is the reason this report publishes no association between tract-level exposure and tract-level prevalence, and the reason the atlas publishes none either. |  |
| Where the atlas shows an exposure layer and a health measure for the same region, both are area aggregates and neither speaks to any individual in it. |  |
